### B. Trust & Reputation in LLM-MAS

**A-Trust**（He et al., ACL 2026, arXiv:2506.02546）
- 6 維度（Grice 1975）：competence / benevolence / integrity / openness / reliability / respect
- attention-based scoring + message/agent 兩種 granularity

**Agentic Trust Framework**（CSA Feb 2026）
- Intern → Junior → Senior → Principal maturity ladder
- 5 gates + demotion

**AgentRank**（Hyperspace 2025-2026）
- PageRank × stake-weighted delegation × Sybil penalty × recency decay

**RepuNet**（arXiv:2505.05029）
- gossip-based
- **LLM agents 90% 偏好 share positive gossip**（negative signal 傳播弱 — 結構性偏誤）

**DRF**（arXiv:2509.05764）
- UCB exploration/exploitation balance
- honesty / capability 兩維分開

**三結構性攻擊：**
- Sybil flooding
- Reputation washing
- Cold start

**Production gap：**
- A2A / MCP / TAP / Fetch.ai 都沒 portable reputation layer
