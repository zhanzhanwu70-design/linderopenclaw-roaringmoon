### Main Insights

1. **Homogeneous flat topology + free-form debate = worst-of-both-worlds**
   - Wastes tokens (2.1-3.4×) AND amplifies sycophancy (85.5%)
   - Must have structured roles + heterogeneity + adaptive stopping

2. **Topology is first-class design axis**
   - SW stabilizes consensus (arXiv:2512.18094)
   - Hierarchical scales better than flat for > 10 agents
   - Graph diffusion (GTD) makes topology task-adaptive

3. **Verification has 3 necessary conditions**
   - Structured roles
   - Heterogeneity (mix temperatures/models/perspectives)
   - Adaptive stopping (not fixed rounds)
